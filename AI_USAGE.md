fine-tuning, and model training is generally permitted under the CloudBasher Research
Source License.

improve an AI/ML model or AI service that is distributed, licensed, hosted for external
users, or otherwise made available outside the organization conducting or commissioning
the training without separate written permission.

Note the shape of that restriction: it is **restricted pending authorization**, not
prohibited outright. Requests are made to <cloudbasher@edleft.org>, and the licence
provides for them — see [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) section 21.

subject to the controlling license terms and the AI provider's treatment of submitted
content.
