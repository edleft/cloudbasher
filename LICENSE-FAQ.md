# CloudBasher License FAQ

This FAQ is explanatory only. If it conflicts with [`LICENSE`](LICENSE), `LICENSE`
controls.

Several answers below are *"not without separate written permission."* That is a
route, not a refusal — see [How do I request permission?](#how-do-i-request-permission)
at the end.

## Can I use CloudBasher for a paid penetration test?
Yes, assuming the testing is authorized and all other license terms are satisfied.

## Can my company use CloudBasher internally?
Yes.

## Can a security vendor use CloudBasher to test its own product?
Yes.

## Can I modify CloudBasher?
Yes, subject to the license.

## Can I fork or redistribute CloudBasher?
Yes, provided the required license, notices, attribution, and restrictions remain intact.

## Can I sell CloudBasher itself?
Not without separate written permission.

## Can I offer CloudBasher itself as SaaS?
Not without separate written permission.

## Can I charge for consulting or managed-security services that use CloudBasher?
Yes. Commercial cybersecurity services are permitted when CloudBasher is used as a tool
rather than sold or licensed as the principal commercial deliverable.

## Can I use an AI coding assistant while working on CloudBasher?
Generally yes. You should not knowingly submit substantial CloudBasher source to a
provider that will use it to train or materially improve an externally available model
in violation of the license.

## Can I train a private internal model using CloudBasher?
Yes, subject to the license.

## Can I fine-tune a local research model?
Yes, if the resulting system remains private/internal.

## Can I later release that model publicly or offer it to customers?
Not without separate written permission if CloudBasher was used for its training or
material improvement.

## Can I use CloudBasher in a public foundation-model training corpus?
No, not without separate written permission.

## Can I train a free, nonprofit, academic, or open-weight model with CloudBasher?
Not if the resulting model will be externally available, unless separately authorized.

## Can I use CloudBasher with private/internal RAG?
Generally yes.

## Can I benchmark an AI system against CloudBasher?
Generally yes. If the benchmark results become a direct optimization or training signal
for an externally available system, the training restriction applies.

## Is CloudBasher open source?
No. It is source-available under a custom use-restricted license.

## How do I request permission?
Email <cloudbasher@edleft.org>, which is the licensing contact in
[`LICENSE`](LICENSE) section 21. Say what you want to do, and with which part of
CloudBasher.

Everywhere this FAQ says *"not without separate written permission,"* the licence means
exactly that and no more: the use is available, and it needs authorization first. There
is no implication that permission will be withheld.

Do not use that address for bug reports or general questions — use the repository's
**Issues** and **Discussions**. Security vulnerabilities have their own private channel,
**Security → Advisories → Report a vulnerability**; see [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).
